A Little About Me

Where Education Meets Technology

I am an academic librarian and Associate Professor at Palm Beach State College, where I support students and faculty through research assistance, library instruction, and professional development.

My career in education has included teaching technology, working as a school library media specialist, and supporting school libraries at the district level. These experiences have shaped my interest in how technology can enhance teaching and learning.

I am also a doctoral candidate in Curriculum and Instruction with a specialization in Educational Technology at the University of Florida. My research interests include generative artificial intelligence, information literacy, and ethical decision-making in higher education.

Behind the Design

AI Design Process

I created this ePortfolio using ChatGPT to generate the HTML and CSS based on my design preferences. Unlike my traditional ePortfolio, which involved modifying an existing template, this version was generated from prompts describing the layout, color scheme, typography, and content I wanted.

I chose to retain the Palm Beach State College green-and-gold color scheme and Libraries logo while exploring a different approach to the design. I selected Playfair Display and Lato for the typography and requested a responsive, single-page layout. After previewing the website in Chrome, I used additional prompts to refine the design, including centering the header.
