Digital Animation - Project 6

For Project 6, I created an animation demonstrating phototropism, the process by which plants grow toward light. This project allowed me to explore how movement and visual storytelling can illustrate scientific concepts and support teaching and learning.

Phototropism Animation

I created this animation to illustrate how plants respond to light. The movement of the plant toward the lamp demonstrates phototropism and shows how animation can make a process that occurs gradually easier to visualize.
