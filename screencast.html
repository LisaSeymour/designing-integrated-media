Screencasting

For the screencasting assignments, I created two instructional videos demonstrating AI features in library research tools. I used Camtasia to produce a traditional screencast and InVideo with Google AI Studio to create an AI-assisted version. Both videos demonstrate tools that can help students conduct academic research.

Traditional Screencast - Camtasia

I used Camtasia to create a screencast demonstrating the AI features available in EBSCO's research platform. The video includes narration, zoom effects, and mouse-click indicators to help students follow along with the demonstration.

View Traditional Screencast

AI-Assisted Screencast - InVideo

For the AI-assisted screencast, I demonstrated Primo's AI Research Assistant. I recorded the screen demonstration myself and used InVideo through its ChatGPT integration to edit the video. I also used Google AI Studio to generate the narration. This project allowed me to explore how AI could assist with instructional video production.
