Lisa Seymour

EME 6208: Designing Integrated Media Environments | University of Florida

Welcome to my digital media portfolio! I am a librarian and Associate Professor at Palm Beach State College and a doctoral candidate in Educational Technology at the University of Florida. My career has included teaching technology, developing school makerspaces, and supporting the integration of emerging technologies into education. This portfolio showcases my work in EME 6208: Designing Integrated Media Environments, including projects in image editing, interactive web design, video production, digital animation, and screencasting. Each project reflects my exploration of digital media tools and their applications in teaching and learning.

Image Editing

For Project 1, I designed two alternative book covers for Remarkably Bright Creatures by Shelby Van Pelt. I used GIMP for traditional image editing and generative AI to create a second design, exploring visual composition, typography, and the differences between traditional and AI-assisted design processes.

Web Design

For Projects 2 and 3, I developed two interactive generative AI literacy activities: a multiple-choice quiz and a drag-and-drop scenario sorting assessment. Using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I explored traditional coding and AI-assisted development to create engaging learning experiences that help students evaluate appropriate uses of generative AI.
