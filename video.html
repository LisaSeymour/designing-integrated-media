Video Production - Project 5

For Project 5, I created two versions of The Case of the Fake Citation, an educational video teaching students how to identify and verify AI-generated citations. I used iMovie for the traditional version and NotebookLM for the AI-generated version, allowing me to compare the two approaches to video production.

Traditional Video Production - iMovie

I created the traditional version using iMovie, combining photographs, recorded narration, captions, and background music. I made the decisions about timing, transitions, and visual elements to create an educational video that uses a detective theme to explain how students can verify citations generated by AI.

Watch Traditionally Created Video

AI-Generated Video Production - NotebookLM

For the AI-generated version, I used NotebookLM to create the video from my script and detailed prompts. NotebookLM generated the narration, illustrations, and visual presentation. This allowed me to explore how generative AI can simplify video production while also limiting the amount of control an instructor has over the finished product.
